# SWYNEX Interactive Cafe Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview

**SWYNEX Interactive Dashboard** is an interactive **Microsoft Power BI** dashboard designed to analyze cafe sales performance across revenue, transactions, sales volume, product performance, payment behavior, location/channel performance, monthly trends, and potential high-value transactions.

The report is built around an embedded **CafeSales** data model with a related **DateTable** and uses Power BI measures and interactive slicers to allow users to move from a high-level performance view to more detailed business insights.

The dashboard contains three report pages:

1. **Dashboard** – Overall sales performance and interactive exploration.
2. **Business Insights** – Product, payment, transaction-type, monthly, and high-value transaction analysis.
3. **Executive Insights** – A consolidated set of management-level findings from the analysis.

---

## Dashboard Objectives

The dashboard is designed to answer the following business questions:

- How much revenue is the cafe generating?
- How many transactions are being recorded?
- How many units are being sold?
- What is the average value of a transaction?
- How many units are sold per transaction on average?
- Which products contribute the most revenue?
- Which products have the highest sales volume?
- How concentrated is revenue across products?
- Which payment method contributes the most revenue?
- How does revenue differ by location/channel?
- How does revenue change month by month?
- Which month generates the highest and lowest revenue?
- Which transactions qualify as potential high-value transactions?
- Which products dominate high-value transaction revenue?
- What proportion of total transactions and revenue comes from high-value transactions?

## Report Pages and Visuals

### 1. Dashboard

The main Dashboard page provides the core sales-performance view.

#### KPI Cards

- **Total Revenue**
- **Total Transactions**
- **Total Quantity**
- **Average Transaction Value**
- **Average Quantity per Transaction**

#### Visuals

- **Monthly Revenue Trend** – Tracks total revenue by month.
- **Revenue by Item** – Compares revenue generated by each product/item.
- **Quantity Sold by Item** – Compares units sold by product/item.
- **Revenue by Payment Method** – Shows the share/contribution of revenue by payment method.
- **Revenue by Location** – Compares revenue across locations/channels.

#### Interactive Slicers

The Dashboard page contains slicers for:

- **Item**
- **Date**
- **Payment Method**
- **Location**
- **Transaction Type**

These slicers dynamically filter the KPI cards and charts, allowing users to investigate specific products, periods, payment methods, locations, or transaction types.

#### Reporting Period

The main dashboard visuals are filtered to the **2023 calendar year**, using a date range from **January 1, 2023 through December 31, 2023** (implemented in the report as `>= 2023-01-01` and `< 2024-01-01`).

---

### 2. Business Insights

The Business Insights page converts the dashboard metrics into more decision-oriented analysis.

#### Visuals and Measures

- **Top Revenue/Quantity** – Identifies the leading product using a Top 1 revenue-based selection.
- **Revenue Concentration by Item** – Shows how total revenue is distributed across products.
- **Transaction Type Distribution** – Shows revenue contribution by transaction type.
- **Payment Method Revenue Contribution** – Compares revenue generated by each payment method.
- **Highest Revenue Month** – Identifies the month with the highest revenue.
- **Lowest Revenue Month** – Identifies the month with the lowest revenue.
- **Potential High-Value Transactions by Item** – Shows high-value transaction concentration by product.
- **High-Value Transactions** – Total count of transactions classified as potential high-value transactions.
- **High-Value Revenue** – Revenue generated by those potential high-value transactions.
- **High-Value Revenue %** – Percentage of total revenue contributed by high-value transactions.

The page uses Top-N logic for the leading revenue item and for the highest/lowest revenue month measures, while the high-value analysis is driven by dedicated Power BI measures.

---

### 3. Executive Insights

The Executive Insights page contains the dashboard's consolidated findings.

## Key Findings

### 1. Overall Performance

The cafe recorded **$89,290.50 in revenue** across **10,000 transactions** and sold **30,271 units**.

### 2. Product Performance

**Juice** was the largest revenue contributor at **$19,075.50**, accounting for **21.36% of total revenue**.

### 3. Revenue Concentration

**Juice, Salad, Sandwich, and Smoothie** together contributed approximately **71.16% of total revenue**.

This indicates that a relatively small group of products accounts for a large portion of overall revenue.

### 4. Sales Volume

**Juice** was also the highest-volume product, with **6,435 units sold**.

### 5. Payment Behavior

**Digital Wallet** generated **$48,354**, representing **54.15% of total revenue**.

### 6. Location Performance

**Takeaway** generated **$62,039**, representing **69.48% of total revenue** and **6,983 transactions**.

### 7. Monthly Performance

**July** generated the highest monthly revenue at **$11,058.50**, approximately **55.5% above the average revenue of the other months**.

### 8. High-Value Transactions

Potential high-value transactions represented only **2.68% of transactions**, but contributed approximately **7.50% of total revenue**.

This shows that the high-value segment is relatively small by transaction count while contributing a disproportionately larger share of revenue.

### 9. High-Value Product Concentration

**Salad** accounted for approximately **92% of the revenue generated by potential high-value transactions**.

### 10. Transaction Value

**Salad** had the highest average transaction value among products at approximately **15.12**.

## Business Problems Addressed

The dashboard provides a single interactive analytical layer for several common cafe/business-performance problems:

### 1. Lack of centralized sales visibility

The KPI cards consolidate revenue, transaction count, units sold, average transaction value, and average quantity per transaction into one management view.

### 2. Difficulty identifying top-performing products

Revenue and quantity-by-item visuals identify which products generate the greatest financial contribution and which products sell the most units.

### 3. Revenue concentration risk

The revenue concentration analysis highlights how dependent overall revenue is on a small group of products.

### 4. Limited understanding of customer payment behavior

Payment-method analysis shows which payment modes contribute the most revenue, with Digital Wallet identified as the dominant contributor in the executive findings.

### 5. Unclear location/channel performance

Revenue-by-location analysis makes it possible to compare performance across locations/channels, with Takeaway identified as the leading contributor in the executive findings.

### 6. Difficulty detecting seasonal or monthly revenue patterns

The monthly revenue trend and highest/lowest month measures reveal periods of stronger and weaker revenue performance.

### 7. Difficulty identifying high-value sales opportunities

Dedicated high-value transaction measures quantify the volume and revenue impact of potential high-value transactions.

### 8. Difficulty understanding which products drive valuable transactions

The high-value transaction-by-item analysis shows which products are associated with the highest-value sales activity, with Salad dominating high-value transaction revenue in the current analysis.

### 9. Static reporting and limited drill-down

Interactive slicers let users change the analytical context without rebuilding charts or reports manually.

### 10. Disconnect between operational metrics and management insights

The Business Insights and Executive Insights pages convert raw performance measures into clearly stated findings that can be communicated to decision-makers.

## Data Model

The Power BI report uses:

- **CafeSales** – Main transactional sales table.
- **DateTable** – Dedicated date dimension used for date filtering and month-based analysis.

The report queries use the DateTable for calendar filtering and month/year analysis, while the CafeSales table provides transaction, item, payment method, location, transaction type, and sales measures.

---

## Interactivity and User Experience

The dashboard is designed for interactive exploration rather than a static report.

Changing a slicer automatically updates the connected measures and visuals. For example, a user can select a particular item or date period and then examine its impact on revenue, quantity, payment method, location performance, and other dashboard metrics.

The available slicers on the main Dashboard page are:

**Item → Date → Payment Method → Location → Transaction Type**

This supports both high-level monitoring and focused analysis of specific business segments.

## Key Management Insights

The most important business-level observations from the current report are:

- Overall performance reached **$89,290.50 revenue**, **10,000 transactions**, and **30,271 units sold**.
- **Juice** is the strongest product in both revenue and unit volume.
- Revenue is highly concentrated: **four products contribute approximately 71.16%** of total revenue.
- **Digital Wallet** is the leading revenue-generating payment method.
- **Takeaway** is the leading location/channel by revenue and transaction count.
- **July** is the strongest month by revenue.
- Potential high-value transactions make up a small share of transactions but a larger share of revenue, indicating meaningful value concentration.
- **Salad** is especially important in the high-value segment, generating approximately **92% of high-value transaction revenue** and having the highest average transaction value at approximately **15.12**.
